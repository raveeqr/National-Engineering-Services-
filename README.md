## Brand Overview

**National Engineering Services** is a Pakistan-based engineering and fabrication company providing customized structural, modular, prefabricated, and outdoor construction solutions for residential, commercial, industrial, institutional, and construction requirements across Pakistan.

### Core Solutions:
1. **Steel Structures**: Heavy industrial sheds, warehouses, factories, and commercial steel frameworks.
2. **Prefabricated Buildings**: Rapid-deployment insulated building systems for site offices and accommodation.
3. **Modular Buildings**: Multi-story and flexible contemporary modular architecture.
4. **Container Offices**: Thermal-insulated, electrified portable workspaces for active job sites.
5. **Canopies & Shades**: Architectural entrance and walkway shade structures.
6. **Gates & Security Solutions**: Industrial sliding gates, cantilever barriers, and perimeter access control.
7. **Parking Shades**: Heavy-duty cantilever vehicle protection structures.
8. **Gazebos & Outdoor Structures**: Custom steel pergolas and recreational structures.

## Homepage Sections Structure

1. **Header & Sticky Navigation**: Custom SVG engineering mark, navigation links, and prominent "GET A QUOTE" CTA.
2. **Hero Section**: Full-width cinematic background photograph of custom steel structure in Pakistan with dual CTAs.

4. **About Us / Introduction**: Two-column layout showcasing company capabilities and engineering philosophy.
5. **Services Grid**: 8 core fabrication and building solutions with interactive technical specification explorer.
6. **Featured Project Showcase**: 6-item portfolio showcase with dark blue hover overlay and interactive lightbox.
7. **Feature Project Spotlight**: Case study spotlight on heavy structural steel execution in Pakistan.
8. **From Concept to Completion**: 4-step streamlined workflow timeline.
9. **Why Choose Us**: Dark navy feature section highlighting reliability, quality, and on-time delivery.
10. **Industries & Applications**: Construction, Industrial, Commercial, Residential, Institutional, and Agricultural sectors.
11. **Pakistan Coverage Section**: Nationwide reach with vector line-art map indicating service delivery to major industrial hubs (Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Karachi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar).
12. **Call to Action**: High-impact full-width conversion banner.
13. **Contact & Lead Generation**: Direct contact cards, prominent WhatsApp button, and technical quote request form.
14. **Footer**: Corporate directory, service index, contact info, and copyright notice.

## Local Development & Preview

### Option 1: Built-in Node.js Server
```bash
node server.js
```
Then open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in your web browser.

### Option 2: Any Static Web Server
You can open `index.html` directly or serve it with Python, Nginx, Apache, or VS Code Live Server.
